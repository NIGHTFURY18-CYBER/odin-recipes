Adobo Manok

Description

Chicken Adobo is a classic Filipino dish made by braising chicken in a savory mix of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

It's a simple one-pot meal that gets better the longer it sits, making it a favorite for both weeknight dinners and leftovers.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken (thighs and legs, cut into pieces)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 1 whole head garlic, crushed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. In a bowl, marinate the chicken in soy sauce, vinegar, and crushed garlic for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and sauté the onion until softened.
  3. Add the marinated chicken (reserve the marinade) and sear on all sides.
  4. Pour in the reserved marinade, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns.

    5. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, covered, for 30 minutes.

  5. Uncover and simmer for another 10-15 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. Season with salt to taste and serve hot with steamed rice.
