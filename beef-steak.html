Beef Steak

Home

Description

Filipino Beef Steak (Bistek) is thin slices of beef marinated in soy sauce and calamansi, then pan-fried and simmered with plenty of onions.

It's a tangy, savory comfort food that pairs perfectly with a bowl of steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 500g beef sirloin, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup calamansi or lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 2 large onions, sliced into rings
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Marinate the beef in soy sauce, calamansi juice, and black pepper for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the garlic until golden.
  3. Remove the beef from the marinade (reserve the marinade) and sear in the pan until browned.
  4. Pour in the reserved marinade and water, then simmer for 10-15 minutes until the beef is tender.

    5. Add the onion rings and cook for another 3-5 minutes until just softened.

  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Serve hot over steamed rice, topped with the onions.
