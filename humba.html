Description

Humba is a Visayan braised pork dish similar to adobo, but sweeter and richer, often cooked with pineapple, black beans, and banana blossoms.

It's a popular dish for special occasions because of its deep, sticky-sweet flavor and tender, fall-apart pork.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly, cut into cubes
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 1/2 cup pineapple chunks (or pineapple juice)
  • 1/4 cup salted black beans (tausi), optional
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil

Steps

  1. Marinate the pork in soy sauce, vinegar, and crushed garlic for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and brown the pork on all sides.
  3. Add the onion and sauté until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the marinade, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns.

    5. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered for about 1 hour, or until the pork is tender.

  5. Add the brown sugar, pineapple, and black beans (if using).
  6. Continue simmering uncovered for 20-30 minutes until the sauce thickens and turns glossy.
  7. Adjust seasoning to taste and serve hot with steamed rice.
